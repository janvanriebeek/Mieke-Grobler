My Fantastic Learning Programs!

Fine Motor Skills (Grades 1–3)

These sessions focus on the small muscle movements needed for writing, cutting, drawing, and everyday classroom tasks. Activities are fun and hands-on, and they build hand-eye coordination, pencil grip, and control. These are key skills for confident early learning.

Maths (Grades 4–6)

I help learners understand core maths concepts such as multiplication, division, fractions, problem-solving, and word sums. Lessons are broken down step by step so maths feels less overwhelming and more enjoyable, with plenty of practice and real-world examples.

Mathematical Literacy (Grades 10–12)

These lessons help learners see how maths applies to everyday life: budgets, interest rates, measurements, and more. I focus on practical problem-solving, clear explanations, and exam preparation so they can walk into this subject with confidence.

Afrikaans and English (All Grades)

Whether your child needs help with reading, writing, grammar, spelling, or comprehension, I offer support in both English and Afrikaans. Lessons are interactive and matched to each learner's level, so they can grow their vocabulary, build stronger sentences, and become more confident communicators.

Exam Preparation and Study Support (All Grades)

I help learners prepare for tests and exams by reviewing key work, building study skills, and working through past papers. We focus on managing time, reading questions carefully, and answering well under pressure.

Hi There! I'm Mieke, Your Learning Friend!

Hi, I'm Mieke Grobler, a passionate and dedicated teacher offering extra classes to learners from Grade 1 to Grade 12. My goal is to support each child's own learning journey by building strong foundations, improving confidence, and encouraging a love of learning.

I have extensive experience working with learners who have ADHD, dyslexia, and autism. I understand how important a calm, supportive, and flexible learning space is. I use strategies that fit each child, such as multi-sensory tools, step-by-step guidance, and a clear routine, so every child can succeed at their own pace.

Whether your child needs help catching up, exam preparation, or ongoing enrichment, I am here to help them reach their potential.
